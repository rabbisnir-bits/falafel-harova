About Us

Our Story

Located in the historic Old City of Jerusalem, Falafel Harova has been serving authentic, homemade falafel for generations. Our family recipe combines traditional techniques with the freshest ingredients to create the perfect falafel every time.

Our Promise

We use only the finest chickpeas, fresh herbs, and traditional spices. Every batch is made fresh throughout the day, ensuring you get the crispiest, most flavorful falafel in Jerusalem. Our vegetables are delivered daily from local markets.

Location

Find us in the Jewish Quarter of the Old City, Jerusalem. Look for our green sign near the main square. Walk through the ancient stone paths and follow the aroma of fresh falafel!
